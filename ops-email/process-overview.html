What It Does

Daily Shift Reports

Pulls every shift leader's submission from yesterday — trade feedback, guest experience, operations notes, rostering, and star of the shift — formatted into a professional HTML email per venue.

Star Champions Leaderboard

Tracks month-to-date "Star of the Shift" nominations and ranks the top 3 team members per venue with Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals.

AI-Powered Insights

Claude Haiku analyses each venue's daily comments and 7-day history to surface key issues and recurring trends — giving managers an instant executive summary.

Venue-Specific Delivery

Each email is sent from the venue's own shared mailbox to configured recipients. Venue lists, senders, and recipients are managed via a simple Excel file on SharePoint.
